## 🎥 SECTION 2: TWO WAYS TO ANALYZE (12-15 minutes)

**Transition:**
> "So that's what the platform captures. Let me show you HOW we capture it - we've built two methods depending on whether you're pitchside or reviewing afterwards."

### A. LIVE MATCH RECORDING (6-7 mins)

**Navigate to:** New Match / Live Recording (or demo an existing live-recorded match in edit mode)

**What to Show:**
1. **Pitch Interface**
   - "This runs on a tablet pitchside during the match"
   - Ball tracking: drag to show player movement
   - Quick actions: scores, turnovers, frees, subs

2. **Ball Carrier Tracking**
   - "Tap the player with the ball, drag to show their run"
   - "Every carry gets tracked - distance, direction, outcome"
   - "These feed into possession chains and transition metrics you saw earlier"

3. **Event Logging**
   - Tap a score/turnover
   - Show how it asks for:
     - Player number
     - Zone on pitch
     - Under pressure? (for xP calculation)
   - "All timestamped, all tied to the video"

4. **Opposition Pass Tracking** (if implemented)
   - "We can track opposition passes to calculate PPDA"
   - Show the tap interface

5. **Half-Time View**
   - If available, show the half-time summary screen
   - "Coaches get live stats at half-time - not after the game"

**Key Message:** *"This works at any level - club or county. One analyst pitchside. No camera array, no vest sensors required. Just an iPad."*

### B. VIDEO TAGGING (6-8 mins)

**Navigate to:** Video Tagging (show an existing video session or start a new one)

**What to Show:**
1. **Setup Flow**
   - "Upload the match video - phone footage, GoPro, whatever you have"
   - Set throw-in time, half-time, attacking direction

2. **Tracking Mode**
   - "Same pitch interface, but synced to video playback"
   - **Key difference:** "Can pause, but once you mark an event, you can't scrub backwards - maintains continuous tracking integrity"
   - Show ball dragging + event logging while video plays

3. **Undo to Point Feature** (NEW - just implemented!)
   - Click "Undo to Point" button
   - "If you make a mistake, scrub back to where you want to fix from"
   - "Shows exactly what events/ball tracking will be deleted"
   - "Confirm, and tracking resumes from that point"

4. **Review Mode**
   - After tracking completes, show "Finish & Review"
   - "Now you can scrub freely, correct any events"
   - **Event Log** - edit, delete, verify events
   - **Sync to Match** - merges video-tagged data with any live recording

5. **Output**
   - "Once synced, this match looks identical to a live-recorded one"
   - "Same charts, same AI reports, same ball carrier data"

**Key Message:** *"Live recording is for pitchside. Video tagging is for deep post-match review when you want to analyze film."*

## ⚠️ POTENTIAL QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

### "How does this compare to Nacsport?"
> "Nacsport is excellent for video analysis and tagging. We're differentiated by:
> - Native GAA tactical metrics (xP, PPDA, ball tracking)
> - AI-powered match reports and insights
> - Integrated live recording + video analysis
> - Player portal and communication tools
> - Built specifically for GAA at all levels - club through inter-county"

### "What about GPS/tracking data?"
> "We integrate GPS (currently support STATSports, Catapult) - you saw training analytics in the dashboard. But the core value is tactical analysis from video/live recording, which works with or without GPS."

### "Can this track opposition players?"
> "Yes - we track opposition passes, footedness, turnovers forced. Currently manual tagging (tap-based), not automated. That's deliberate - gives your analyst control over what's captured."

### "How long does video tagging take?"
> "Roughly real-time to 1.5x match length, depending on detail level. So a 60-minute match takes 60-90 minutes to fully tag. Live recording is faster but requires you to be pitchside."

### "What if we already use [other tool]?"
> "OneStat is designed to complement, not replace. If you're using Nacsport for video breakdown, OneStat can import your tagged events. If you're using StatSports for GPS, we integrate that data. The question is whether our GAA-specific tactical metrics and AI insights add value to what you're already doing."
