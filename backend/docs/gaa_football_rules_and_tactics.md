# GAA Football - Rules, Tactics, and Tactical Knowledge Base

## Official Rules & Scoring

### Scoring System
- **Goal**: Ball under the crossbar = 3 points
- **Point**: Ball over the crossbar between the posts = 1 point
- **45-meter free**: 1 point (taken from 45m line after goalkeeper saves/short shot)
- **Two-pointer**: 2 points for scores (points, not goals) from play taken outside the marked arc (approximately 40m+ from goal). In use since 2023-2024 season in inter-county competitions.

### Game Structure
- **Duration**: 60-70 minutes depending on level (30-35 min per half at senior inter-county)
- **Teams**: 15 players per side (1 goalkeeper + 14 outfield)
- **Substitutions**: Rolling subs allowed (5-6 depending on competition)
- **Restarts**: 
  - Kickout after score/wide/45
  - Throw-in at start of match and second half ONLY
  - Sideline ball for ball out of play

### Ball Handling Rules
- **Possession**: Player may take max 4 steps while holding the ball
- **Solo**: Bounce or toe-tap the ball back to hand to reset steps
- **Hand pass**: Strike ball with open hand (fist pass allowed)
- **Foot pass**: Kick from hands or ground
- **Pick up**: Must use foot to lift ball from ground (not hands directly)

### Fouls & Cards
- **Yellow Card**: Caution (player stays on)
- **Black Card**: 10 minutes in sin-bin (can be replaced by sub)
- **Red Card**: Sent off, cannot be replaced
- **Foul count**: NO automatic card threshold - entirely referee discretion
- Common fouls: Overcarrying (>4 steps), square ball (pass forward), contact fouls

### The Square
- 14m x 14m box around goal
- Attacking team cannot score if player in square before ball
- Defensive team: goalkeeper protected from contact

## Pitch Zones & Spatial Concepts

### Standard Pitch Dimensions
- Length: ~130-145m (variations exist)
- Width: ~80-90m
- **45m line**: Critical tactical boundary (free kicks from here are scoreable)
- **20m line**: Defensive structure line

### Tactical Zones (Vertical - Length)
1. **Defensive Third** (Own goal to ~35m out)
   - Goalkeeper + 6 defenders typically
   - Protect goal, win turnovers, launch attacks
   
2. **Midfield Third** (~35m to ~75m)
   - Contest kickouts, control tempo
   - High-value turnover zone (win it here → score opportunity)
   
3. **Attacking Third** (~75m to opposition goal)
   - Inside 45m = prime scoring zone
   - Breaking down defensive structure

### Lateral Channels (Width)
- **Central channel**: Most direct route, highest defensive concentration
- **Left/Right channels**: Width to stretch defenses
- **Corner zones**: Low conversion but can exploit poor defensive positioning

## Core Tactical Concepts

### 1. Kickout Strategy

**Own Kickouts**
- **Short kickout** (inside 45m): Retain possession safely, slower build-up
  - Risk: High press can force turnover in dangerous area
  - Reward: Controlled possession in own half
  
- **Long kickout** (beyond 50m): Contest in midfield/attacking territory
  - Risk: 50/50 battle, can lose possession
  - Reward: Win in attacking position, immediate scoring threat
  
- **Targeting**: Identify dominant aerial players (e.g., Jason McGee)
- **Vary strategy**: Alternate short/long to keep opposition guessing

**Opposition Kickouts**
- **Press short kickouts**: Force error in their defensive third
- **Contest long kickouts**: Midfielders/forwards battle for possession
- **Win rate**: >40% is dominant, <30% is poor
- **Key metric**: PPDA (Passes Per Defensive Action) - fewer opposition passes before winning ball = sharper press

### 2. Pressing Systems

**High Press**
- Push up beyond opposition 45m, deny space
- Force turnovers in their defensive/midfield third
- High risk (leaves space in behind), high reward (win ball near their goal)
- Requires fitness, discipline, coordinated triggers

**Mid-Block Press**
- Sit around halfway line, compact middle third
- Deny through-balls, force opposition wide or back
- Balanced approach

**Deep Block / Blanket Defence**
- All 14 outfield players behind the ball
- Deny space inside 45m, force long-range shots
- Counter-attack on turnovers
- Used when protecting a lead or outmatched

**Press Trigger Examples**
- Goalkeeper receiving ball → sprint to close down short kickout
- Sideways pass in midfield → coordinated squeeze
- Player isolated near sideline → trap and win ball

### 3. Possession Football

**Patient Build-Up**
- Retain ball through passes, hand-passes, lateral movement
- Draw opposition out of shape, exploit gaps
- Requires composure, support runners, off-ball movement

**Risk**: Turnover in own half → immediate counter-attack threat

**Key principles**:
- Support angles (always 2-3 passing options)
- Move opposition (shift their defensive line)
- Patience until shooting opportunity opens

### 4. Direct Football / Counter-Attack

**Philosophy**: Win ball → move it forward quickly before opposition resets

**Execution**:
1. Win turnover (anywhere on pitch)
2. Immediate forward pass or carry
3. Exploit disorganized opposition defense
4. Shoot within 3-5 passes

**Metrics**:
- Turnover-to-shot time: Elite = <10 seconds
- Ball recovery time: <2 min average = aggressive
- Score-per-possession from turnovers: >30% = clinical

### 5. Contact vs Avoidance

**Contact Football**
- Carry into contact, draw foul, win free kick
- Physical dominance, test opposition discipline
- Risk: Lose possession in contact, opposition gains momentum

**Contact Avoidance**
- Quick hand-passing, off-ball movement
- "Keep the ball alive" - rapid transfers
- Denies opposition chance to tackle
- Requires fitness, technical skill, spatial awareness

**Modern trend**: Top teams minimize contact through quick ball movement, only carry into contact when free kick in scoring range is valuable

### 6. Transition Play

**Defensive Transition** (Lose ball → defend)
- Immediate pressure on ball carrier (counter-press)
- Drop behind ball quickly
- Deny space for opposition counter

**Offensive Transition** (Win ball → attack)
- Rapid forward movement
- Exploit opposition out of shape
- Decision: Go direct or retain and build?

**Key stat**: Ball recovery time (how long to win ball back after losing it)

### 7. Set Pieces

**45-Meter Free (45)**
- Worth 1 point
- Awarded when goalkeeper saves shot or ball goes short/wide
- Taken from 45m line in front of goal
- High-percentage scoring opportunity
- 70-80% conversion expected from elite free-taker

**Penalty**
- Worth 3 points (goal)
- Awarded for foul in the square or certain fouls denying goal-scoring opportunity
- Taken from ground directly in front of goal, no defenders
- Must score - anything less is a miss

**Sideline Ball**
- Restart near touchline
- Quick plays can exploit defensive lapses

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) - Tactical

### Scoring Efficiency
- **Shot Efficiency**: (Scores ÷ Total shots) × 100
  - >50% = excellent
  - <35% = wasteful
  
- **Score-per-possession**: (Scores ÷ Possessions) × 100
  - >30% = elite attacking efficiency
  - <22% = attack dysfunction

- **From-play percentage**: (Scores from play ÷ Total scores) × 100
  - >60% = strong open-play attack
  - <45% = over-reliant on frees

### Possession Quality
- **Unforced errors per game**:
  - <5 = disciplined
  - >8 = careless
  
- **Turnover differential**: (Won - Lost)
  - Positive = winning possession battle
  - Negative = losing ball more than winning

### Restart Battle
- **Own kickout retention**: 
  - >65% = secure
  - <50% = vulnerable
  
- **Opposition kickout win rate**:
  - >40% = dominant
  - <30% = letting them restart easily

### Defensive Solidity
- **Points conceded from play** (per game):
  - <5 = excellent defensive structure
  - >8 = porous defense

- **Clean sheet rate**: % of games conceding 0 goals
  - >50% = exceptional
  - <30% = concern

- **Goals conceded per game**:
  - <0.7 = excellent
  - >1.2 = defensive crisis

### Discipline
- **Fouls per game**:
  - <12 = disciplined
  - >15 = indiscipline problem (but NO automatic card rule)
  
- **Cards per game**:
  - <1.0 = disciplined
  - >2.0 = systemic issue

## Spatial Tactical Patterns

### Zone-Based Analysis

**Scoring Zones - Conversion Rates**
- **Inside 45m, central**: 60-75% (prime zone)
- **Inside 45m, flanks**: 40-55%
- **Outside 45m**: 15-30% (speculative)
- **Corners**: 20-35% (tight angles)

**Tactical insight**: Teams shooting >30% from outside 45m are wasting possession

**Turnover Patterns**
- **Defensive third turnovers lost**: Immediate danger → opposition scores within 2 minutes
- **Midfield turnovers lost**: Controllable → regroup defensively
- **Attacking third turnovers lost**: Acceptable risk → opposition far from goal

**Winning ball in zones**:
- **Win in defensive third**: Safe but must build from deep
- **Win in midfield third**: Balanced position, transition opportunity
- **Win in attacking third**: Gold - immediate scoring chance

### Inside vs Outside 45m

**Inside 45m**
- All scoring attempts here are valid tactical choices
- Defending team protects this space intensely
- Most scores originate here

**Outside 45m**
- Shots from here = low conversion, wasted possession
- Only justified if: free kick, end of half, no better option
- Tactical error: Rushing shots from outside 45m

## Modern Tactical Trends (2020s)

### 1. Aggressive Pressing
- Teams press higher up the pitch than ever
- PPDA (passes allowed before defensive action) tracked
- Lower PPDA = more aggressive press

### 2. Goalkeeper as Playmaker
- Kickouts are possession starts, not just clearances
- Vary short/long, target specific runners
- Elite teams retain 70%+ of own kickouts

### 3. Ball Retention Over Territory
- Modern teams value keeping ball over gaining ground
- Patient build-up until opportunity emerges
- Avoid "turnover in contact" mistakes

### 4. Data-Driven Substitutions
- Monitor fatigue via GPS
- Identify players "spiking" (performing above baseline)
- Replace struggling players proactively (3+ errors = sub consideration)

### 5. Position-Specific Roles
- **Sweeper**: Extra defender behind full-back line
- **Roaming midfielder**: Drops deep to receive, carries forward
- **Target forward**: Wins kickouts, holds up play
- **Pressing forward**: Denies opposition kickout options

## Common Tactical Mistakes

### Shooting
1. **Shooting from outside 45m**: <25% conversion, wasted possession
2. **Rushing shots under no pressure**: Unforced wide/short
3. **Not working the ball closer**: Settling for difficult angle when better option exists

### Possession
1. **Overcarrying into contact**: Losing ball in dangerous areas
2. **Lateral passing with no purpose**: Not moving opposition
3. **No support runners**: Ball carrier has no options

### Defensive
1. **Not tracking runners**: Opposition player free in space
2. **Ball-watching**: Focused on ball, not marking assignment
3. **Leaving space in behind**: High press without cover

### Restarts
1. **Predictable kickout strategy**: Always short or always long
2. **Not pressing opposition kickouts**: Letting them build from their kickout
3. **Poor kickout retention**: <50% = giving opposition easy possession

## Analyzing Match Flow

### Momentum Shifts (Detect via data)
- **Scoring run**: 3+ scores without reply → momentum with that team
- **Scoring drought**: 10+ minutes no score → stagnant attack
- **Turnover spike**: 5+ turnovers in 10 min → team rattled

### Critical Moments
- **Sin-bin**: 10 min at 14 vs 15 → defending team must survive
- **Substitution impact**: Sub enters → immediate improvement/decline?
- **Formation change**: Tactical tag shows shift → did it work?

### Spatial Dominance
- **Territory**: Which third was ball in most?
- **Turnover map**: Where are we winning/losing ball?
- **Scoring origins**: Are scores from open play or frees?

## Half-Time Adjustments (What Managers Do)

### Tactical Changes
1. **Change kickout strategy** (if retention poor)
2. **Switch press intensity** (if tiring or opposition adapting)
3. **Target opposition weakness** (e.g., weak left flank)
4. **Shore up defense** (if conceding too easily)

### Personnel Changes
1. **Sub struggling player** (3+ errors, multiple wides)
2. **Fresh legs in midfield** (kickout battle fatigue)
3. **Defensive reinforcement** (if losing by significant margin)

### Messaging
1. **Reinforce what's working** (e.g., "Our press is winning ball, keep it up")
2. **Address errors** (e.g., "Stop shooting from outside 45m")
3. **Motivate** (e.g., "We're one goal away, stay composed")

## Position-Specific Expectations

### Goalkeeper
- Kickout retention >65%
- Shot-stopping (saves/total shots faced)
- NOT expected to score from play
- Free-taking role acceptable (scores from frees/45s valued)

### Defenders (Full-backs, Half-backs, Sweeper)
- Turnovers won > turnovers lost
- Minimal unforced errors in own half
- Block shots, deny space
- NOT expected to score heavily (0-2 points/game acceptable)

### Midfielders
- Win kickouts (aerial dominance)
- High work rate (carries, tackles, passes)
- Score contribution (2-4 points/game)
- Transition play (win ball → move forward)

### Forwards (Half-forwards, Full-forwards)
- Score heavily (4-8 points/game)
- Shot efficiency >50%
- Create space for others
- Press opposition kickouts
- Win frees in scoring positions

## GPS & Physical Benchmarks

### Elite Inter-County Standards (Per 70min match)
- **Total distance**: 10-13km
- **High-speed running** (>20km/h): 1.5-2.5km
- **Sprints** (>25km/h): 400-800m
- **Accelerations/decelerations**: 150-250 per match

### Positional Differences
- **Midfielders**: Highest total distance (12-13km)
- **Forwards**: Highest sprint distance (700-900m)
- **Defenders**: High-intensity efforts (200-250 accel/decel)

### Fatigue Indicators
- **Distance drop-off**: >10% decline second half = fatigue
- **High-speed decline**: >20% drop = "heavy legs"
- **Repeat sprint ability**: Can they sprint again within 60s?

## Opposition Analysis

### Scouting Framework
1. **Kickout patterns**: Do they go short or long? Who do they target?
2. **Scoring threats**: Who takes frees? Who scores from play?
3. **Defensive system**: High press, mid-block, or deep block?
4. **Weaknesses**: Do they concede turnovers in specific zones?
5. **Footedness**: Are key players left or right-footed? (Force onto weaker side)

### In-Match Exploitation
- **Adapt to their kickout**: If they go short, press. If long, contest.
- **Target weak defenders**: Isolate slower full-back
- **Exploit fatigue**: Second-half surges when they tire

## Terminology Glossary

- **Breaking ball**: Loose ball after kickout/contest
- **Solo**: Bounce/toe-tap to reset possession steps
- **Carry**: Running with ball in hand
- **Hand-pass**: Striking ball off hand to teammate
- **Foot-pass**: Kicking ball
- **Dummy solo**: Fake a solo to deceive opponent
- **Overlap**: Runner goes outside ball carrier to receive pass
- **Inside runner**: Runner cuts inside for pass
- **Sweeper**: Extra defender behind back line
- **Mark**: One-on-one defensive assignment
- **Zone**: Defending space, not a specific opponent
- **Screen**: Block passing lane without fouling
- **Blanket defense**: All players behind ball
- **Counter-attack**: Rapid attack after winning turnover
- **Transition**: Shift from defense to attack (or vice versa)

## Agent Tactical Analysis Framework

When analyzing match data, agents should:

### Spatial Analysis (REQUIRED)
- Use `pitch_x` and `pitch_y` coordinates for EVERY tactical insight
- Name specific zones (e.g., "defensive left channel", "inside-45 center")
- Never say "certain areas" or "some zones" - be precise

### Player-Specific Insights (REQUIRED)
- Name players for every claim (errors, turnovers, scores, cards)
- Compare to their own baseline (not just squad average)
- Note position context (don't expect goalkeeper to score from play)

### Tactical Context (REQUIRED)
- Reference formation snapshots, tactical tags, press triggers
- Explain WHY a pattern matters (e.g., "turnovers in defensive third led to 2 opposition scores")
- Connect events to outcomes (e.g., "Gallagher's 3 turnovers won in midfield started 2 scoring attacks")

### Chart Integration (REQUIRED)
- Explicitly reference what charts show in narrative analysis
- Don't isolate insights from visual data
- Example: "The Turnover Map confirms we dominated midfield (+4 turnovers won) but struggled in our defensive left channel"

### Evidence-Based Claims (REQUIRED)
- Never infer tactics without data (e.g., don't claim "going short" without kickout landing zones)
- If data doesn't exist, say "data not available" instead of guessing
- All claims must trace to specific events, stats, or spatial coordinates
