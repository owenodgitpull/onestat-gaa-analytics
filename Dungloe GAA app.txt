Project Overview                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                               
  What it is:                                                                                                                                  
  A real-time match recording and analytics application for Gaelic Athletic Association (GAA) football matches, specifically built for Dungloe 
  GAA Club in County Donegal, Ireland.                                                                                                         
                                                                                                                                               
  Who it's for:                                                                                                                                
  - Match recorders/analysts who track live GAA matches on iPad                                                                                
  - Coaches who need post-match analysis and statistics                                                                                        
  - Team management for historical match data and player performance tracking                                                                  
                                                                                                                                               
  Primary Use Case:   
End Goal Features
1. Live Match Recording: Real-time event logging during matches
1. Time-Based Possession Tracking: Accurate possession percentages based on duration
1. Player Performance Analytics: Individual player statistics across matches
1. Team Analytics: Match statistics, kickout success rates, turnovers, shot maps
1. AI Integration: Claude AI providing live match insights, post-match analysis, and player-specific recommendations
1. Dashboard: Comprehensive views for matches, players, team performance over time - gets better over the season, will train an LLM on all things GAA tactics by using a docs knowledge base, then also providing Dungloe specific knowledge about the team.
                                                                                                                         
  Live match recording on iPad with touch-based interface for tracking every event (scores, turnovers, fouls, kickouts, etc.) with precise     
  pitch positioning and real-time statistics.

  GAA Football Context                                                                                                                         
                                                                                                                                               
  Scoring System:                                                                                                                              
  - Goal = 3 points (ball into net)                                                                                                            
  - Point = 1 point (ball over crossbar, inside 40m arc)                                                                                       
  - 2-Pointer = 2 points (ball over crossbar, outside 40m arc - "45m+ rule")                                                                   
  - Final score format: "2-14" means 2 goals + 14 points = 20 total points                                                                     
                                                                                                                                               
  Match Structure:                                                                                                                             
  - 2 halves of 30 minutes each                                                                                                                
  - 15 players per team (1 GK, 6 backs, 2 midfielders, 6 forwards)                                                                             
  - 5 substitutes on bench                                                                                                                     
  - Kickouts after every score (from goalkeeper)                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                               
  Key Positions (Lineup Format):                                                                                                               
  - GK (Goalkeeper)                                                                                                                            
  - CB (Corner Back), FB (Full Back) - 3 defenders                                                                                             
  - HB (Half Back), CHB (Centre Half Back) - 3 defenders                                                                                       
  - MF (Midfielder) - 2 players                                                                                                                
  - HF (Half Forward), CHF (Centre Half Forward) - 3 attackers                                                                                 
  - CF (Corner Forward), FF (Full Forward) - 3 attackers

  ✅ Interactive GAA Pitch Visualization                                                                                                       
  - SVG-based pitch with accurate GAA dimensions (145m x 90m aspect ratio)                                                                     
  - Touch-enabled ball positioning (tap anywhere to move ball)                                                                                 
  - Visual 40m arc zones for 2-pointer detection                                                                                               
  - Real-time ball possession tracking (Dungloe/Opponent)                                                                                      
  - Color-coded ball: Blue (Dungloe), Red (Opponent)                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                               
  ✅ Match Management                                                                                                                          
  - Create/start/complete matches                                                                                                              
  - Match phases: not_started → first_half → half_time → second_half → finished                                                                
  - Live timer with minute/second tracking                                                                                                     
  - Half-time controls with automatic pause at 30 minutes                                                                                      
  - Score tracking (goals and points separately for both teams)                                                                                
                                                                                                                                               
  ✅ Event Recording System                                                                                                                    
  - Scoring: Goals, Points, 2-Pointers, Wides, Saved shots, Short shots                                                                        
  - Free Kicks: Point (Free), 2-Pointer (Free), Wide (Free)                                                                                    
  - Turnovers: Turnover Won/Lost, Unforced Errors (Our/Opponent)                                                                               
  - Kickouts: 8 variants tracking our/opp kickouts, clean wins, break ball wins                                                                
  - Fouls: Foul Won, Foul Committed with context-aware player selection                                                                        
  - Cards: Yellow Card, Red Card                                                                                                               
  - Defense: Blocks, Interceptions                                                                                                             
  - Substitutions: Full player swap tracking with field status updates                                                                         
  - All events stored with: player attribution, pitch coordinates (x/y), minute, half, team                                                    
                                                                                                                                               
  ✅ Starting Lineup Management                                                                                                                
  - Interactive pitch visualization with 15 position jerseys + 5 substitute slots                                                              
  - Click jersey icon to select/deselect players                                                                                               
  - Proper GAA position labels (GK, CB, FB, HB, CHB, MF, HF, CHF, CF, FF)                                                                      
  - Substitutes displayed below pitch (separate from playing area)                                                                             
  - X button next to each player in list to remove                                                                                             
  - "Use Last Match Lineup" button for quick setup                                                                                             
  - Lineup saved to database tied to match for historical analysis                                                                             
  - "Start First Half" button disabled until lineup selected                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                               
  ✅ Substitution System                                                                                                                       
  - Substitution event type in manual entry modal                                                                                              
  - Two-player selection: player coming off (from field), player coming on (from bench)                                                        
  - Second dropdown disabled until first player selected                                                                                       
  - Automatic is_on_field status tracking in database                                                                                          
  - Substitution validation: can't select player off who isn't on field                                                                        
  - Field status updates automatically after substitution recorded                                                                             
                                                                                                                                               
  ✅ Foul Recording Modal

    - Opponent fouls: "Who was fouled?" (select Dungloe player)                                                                                
    - Dungloe fouls: "Who committed it?" (select Dungloe player)

  - Free kick scoring tracked separately (Point Free, 2PT Free, Wide Free)                                                                     
                                                                                                                                               
  ✅ Manual Event Entry Modal                                                                                                                  
  - Retrospective event logging for missed events                                                                                              
  - Team selection (Dungloe/Opponent)                                                                                                          
  - Event type dropdown (organized by category)                                                                                                
  - Conditional player selection based on event type                                                                                           
  - Minute and half selection                                                                                                                  
  - Supports all event types including substitutions

  Design Patterns & Code Quality                                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                               
  Good Practices Implemented:                                                                                                                  
  - TypeScript throughout frontend for type safety                                                                                             
  - Async/await pattern consistently used                                                                                                      
  - React Query for server state management (caching, optimistic updates)                                                                      
  - Component composition (modals, buttons, pitch as separate components)                                                                      
  - Glassmorphism UI design for modern, professional look                                                                                      
  - Enum-based event types prevent typos/errors                                                                                                
  - UUID primary keys for distributed system compatibility                                                                                     
  - CORS properly configured for development                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                               
  Areas for Improvement:                                                                                                                       
  - No frontend error boundaries yet                                                                                                           
  - Limited loading states (some queries don't show loading UI)                                                                                
  - No database migrations system (using auto-create tables)                                                                                   
  - No automated tests (frontend or backend)                                                                                                   
  - API responses not paginated (could be issue with large datasets)                                                                           
  - No rate limiting on API                                                                                                                    
  - Sensitive data in git history (GitHub token in remote URL)

  Project Context & User Workflow                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                               
  Typical Match Recording Session:                                                                                                             
  1. Create new match (opponent, date, venue)                                                                                                  
  2. Select starting lineup (15 starters + 5 subs)                                                                                             
  3. Start First Half (timer begins)                                                                                                           
  4. Record events in real-time:                                                                                                               
    - Tap pitch to move ball                                                                                                                   
    - Select event type from categorized buttons                                                                                               
    - Choose player when prompted                                                                                                              
    - System auto-updates score, stats, recent events                                                                                          
  5. Half-time break (automatic at 30 minutes)                                                                                                 
  6. Start Second Half                                                                                                                         
  7. Continue recording events                                                                                                                 
  8. Complete match (manual button or auto at 60 minutes)                                                                                      
  9. Review statistics and event history                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                               
  Manual corrections:                                                                                                                          
  - Delete incorrect events via recent events list                                                                                             
  - Add missed events via Manual Entry modal                                                                                                   
  - Record substitutions via Manual Entry (player off → player on)
