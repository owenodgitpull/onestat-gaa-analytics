**Where Dublin scored from.** Dublin scored 19 from 32 inside the 45 centre (59%). We scored 16 from 23 in the same zone (70%) from the zone summary. Dublin's kickouts were the biggest source: C. Kilkenny won 6 and T. Clancy won 5.

**Kickouts.** Our kickout retention was 21 of 39 across the match (54%), below the 60% target. The phase scorecard shows 21 kept and 18 lost. The 1st half kept 6 of 12, the 2nd half 10 of 17, extra time 1 2 of 6 and extra time 2 3 of 4. The knowledge base notes that kickouts originate a third of possessions in a match (GAA Tactical Periodisation presentation, p.6), so the 18 we lost fed a large share of Dublin's 51 possessions.

**Turnovers.** We won 13 turnovers and lost 12 (summary). In the zone count, we won 5 and lost 0 in defensive centre, but lost 7 and won 1 in attacking centre. That is the clearest place we gave the ball away.

**Ball movement.** We carried 332 times from 284 logged passes. Of 47 chains, 19 scored, 11 ended in a turnover, 13 in a missed shot and 4 in other endings. ## 7. Training Recommendations

1. **Own kickout routines.** Build the session around the whole-match figure of 21 kept from 39. Rehearse medium and short options to a named target in a live press, with a rotating restart so our kickout taker practises each length and side, and track retention against the 60% target. This follows from the 18 kickouts lost that became 9 of Dublin's 25 scores.
2. **Contesting their kickouts.** Run a break-ball session on Dublin's kickout landing zones, with our midfield and half-forward line set up as at the 1st-half average (7.8 players in their half at the kick, 76.1 m from our goal). Measure the share of their kickouts we win against 7 of 33 in this match. A suggested aim is to improve on that, using the 1st-half 4 of 13 as the best period to beat.
3. **Transition to defence.** Use a lose-it, win-it style game (Coaching Defensive Play in Gaelic Football, p.5, 7) where the team that loses the ball must switch to mark runners rather than chase the ball player. It follows from 8 of 10 GPS-tracked turnovers catching us short, with a median 8.0 seconds to get enough players goal-side. A suggested target is to get under that 8.0-second median.
4. **Ball security in the final third.** Run possession drills inside the 40m arc with a turnover count, tied to the 7 turnovers lost in attacking centre against 1 won.
