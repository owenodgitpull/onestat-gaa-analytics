**Match Summary**

**What mattered most:** (1) Dublin won the match on our kickouts: 18 of ours were lost and became 9 Dublin scores (14 points), out of 25 Dublin scores in all. (2) We led by 2 points at half-time (2-07 to 1-08), were level after 70 minutes (2-18 each) and lost extra time by 3 points, 0-04 to 0-07. (3) Dublin had the extra range as well as the extra shots: 3 two-pointers to our 4, but 42 shots to our 34.

Donegal lost to Dublin by 4 points (2-22 to 2-26) away from home, after extra time. Ryan McHugh began on the bench, the first time this season he hasn't started. He came on at half-time for Max Campbell and finished with 0-3.

**How the Game Was Lost**

**Lost by 4 points (2-22 to 2-26). It was level at the end of normal time and decided in extra time, where Dublin won the first period by 3 points and the second by 1.**

**What decided it.** The largest gap was our own kickouts. We kept 21 and lost 18. Of the 18 that became Dublin possessions, 15 ended in a shot and 9 in a score (14 points), the most productive phase of the match for either side. Dublin scored 6 of their 7 extra-time scores from phases we could have influenced: 4 of the 5 possessions from our lost kickouts in extra time ended in a score. Separately, our own kept kickouts produced 7 scores (11 points) from 21 possessions, so the ball was worth scoring from when we held it.

**When it turned.** We were never more than 4 points up (1st half 20') and led for most of normal time. Dublin's 1st half 21'-30' block (1-04 to 0-02) took them ahead before Mogan's goal at 1st half 34' put us 2 up again. The game then swung back and forth: they levelled at 2nd half 52', 56' and 62'. Dublin's goal by P. Small at 2nd half 65' put them 2 up, and Michael Murphy's two-point free at 2nd half 70+1' levelled it. In extra time Dublin outscored us 5 points to 2, then 3 to 2. We never led in extra time.

Over the match, our left channel in the first half kept 1 of 5, while our right kept 4 of 5; in the second half the centre kept 3 of 8. The second-half pattern is a pointer rather than a rule, but the first-half left-channel figure is worth looking at before choosing where to kick. Second, getting players goal-side after we lose it: our median recovery was 9 seconds, and in 9 of 10 tracked turnovers we were caught short. Third, who picks up their scorers: C. Basquel finished with 0-7 and C. O'Callaghan with 1-5.

**Tactical Analysis**

**Two-pointers.** From play, we scored 2 (Shane O'Donnell at 2nd half 53', Jason McGee at 2nd half 61') and from frees 2 (Michael Murphy at 2nd half 70+1', Gavin Mulreany at extra time 1 80'), 4 in total, worth 8 points. Dublin scored 3 from play (C. O'Callaghan at 1st half 1', C. Basquel at 2nd half 62', S. Guiden at extra time 1 75') and none from frees, worth 6 points. We gained from range, by 2 points; it did not decide the match, which turned on kickouts and extra-time scoring.

**Kickouts.** We kept 21 of 39 kickouts, and Dublin turned our 18 lost ones into 9 scores (14 points). The principle: in Gaelic football, kick-outs are the origin of possession for 29-33% of all possessions in a match (GAA Tactical Periodisation Model, p.6). Our figures fit that: 39 of our 90-odd kickouts-and-possessions were restarts, and what we did with them decided the match. By length, medium kicks to midfield kept 2 of 7 in the first half but 6 of 9 in the second half. Short kickouts into our defensive third kept 3 of 3 in extra time 2.

**Who won it at midfield.** Dublin's C. Kilkenny won 6 kickouts, T. Clancy 5, and B. Howard and L. Gannon 4 each. We won 7 of their kickouts in the match and 1 of their 10 in the second half. The knowledge base notes a kickout-contest strategy needs support players positioned around the jump to win the break (GAA Tactical Periodisation Model, p.8). Our GPS shows us pushing up on their kickouts: 8.8 to 9.3 players in their half in the first and second halves.

**Ball movement.** Our 332 logged carries produced 47 chains: 19 scoring chains (7 direct, 12 build-up), 11 ending in a turnover and 13 ending in a missed shot, with 4 other endings. Chains averaged 7.1 carriers and scoring chains 7.2, against 5.1 for turnover chains. Most carriers made positive ground, led by Caolan McGonagle (15.9 metres per carry over 15 carries) and Hugh McFadden (16.6 over 6 carries, a small sample). Dublin's chains averaged the same 7.1 carriers, with 24 scoring chains from 57 (6 direct, 18 build-up). Dublin's carriers started and ended deep in their own half: C. Kilkenny (59 carries) gained 4.6 metres each, and their possessions from the ball path held the ball for a median of 22 seconds, with 34 of 58 built into our defensive third (15 via our right, 11 via the centre, 8 via our left). Tempo was similar: 5.3 seconds between carriers for us against 5.8 for Dublin.

**Zones.** We scored 16 of 23 from the inside-45 centre (70%), 4 of 8 from the inside-45 right, and 0 of 2 from the inside-45 left. Dublin scored 19 of 32 in the same centre zone. Our turnover battle was won in the defensive centre (5 won, 0 lost) but lost in the attacking centre (1 won, 7 lost).

**Forwards dropping deep.** Our forwards did not drop especially deep, apart from the link men: Shane O'Donnell and Langan picked up on average 55 m and 52 m from their goal. Hugh McFadden at midfield averaged 90 m from their goal on 6 carries, a small sample. Mogan, listed at corner back, averaged 61 m. The knowledge base on defensive play stresses forwards closing down the backs and not drifting after the ball (Coaching Defensive Play, p.5), which is a point about our shape that the GPS section picks up.

**Personnel and position changes.** McHugh's half-time introduction for Campbell was followed by 3 points from 3 scores for us in the next 10 minutes of the second half, against 3 points from 3 scores for Dublin (before the change: 4 points from 2 scores to 5 points from 3). That's a small sample, so it can't be called a success or a failure. Late in the match, Ó Baoill came on for Martin at extra time 1 70' and Ó Baoill and Langan swapped between centre half forward and midfield at extra time 1 71'. In the 10 minutes after that, we scored 2 points from 1 score to their 4 points from 3 scores; the data can't say why.

**Areas for Improvement**

**Kickouts.** Dublin scored 9 times (14 points) from the 18 kickouts we lost, against 7 scores (11 points) from the 21 we kept. We kept 6 of 12 in the first half, 10 of 17 in the second half and 2 of 6 in the first period of extra time. The first half left channel kept 1 of 5 and the right 4 of 5; in the second half the left kept 5 of 7 and the centre 3 of 8. This is the biggest-sample finding, so the length and side of our kickouts is the first thing to review.

**Getting back after a turnover.** In the GPS measure, 9 of 10 tracked turnovers caught us short, with a median recovery of 9 seconds and 4 not recovered within 15 seconds. This applies to the whole match, not one half.

**Picking up their scorers.** C. Basquel (0-7) and C. O'Callaghan (1-5) scored 12 of Dublin's 32 points between them. In 13 of their 22 GPS-tracked scores, nobody of ours was within 5 m of the tagged spot. Who picks up each of them is a decision worth making.

**Discipline.** We conceded 11 frees, and Dublin scored from 2 of them. Murphy gave away 3 fouls (obstruction, pushing and pulling) and McHugh 2 (pulling and overcarrying). Both Murphy and McGee took black cards at extra time 1 70'.

**Ball security in the attacking centre.** We lost 7 turnovers there against 1 won, and Langan had 2 stray passes in the attacking half (1st half 17' and extra time 2 87').

**Training Recommendations**

1. **Kickout variety and target.** Our kickout retention fell to 2 of 6 in the first period of extra time, and over the whole match we kept 21 of 39. Work on the length and side we choose, starting from the whole-match split (medium kicks to midfield kept 8 of 16 across the two normal-time halves, short kickouts 4 of 5).
2. **Transition to defence.** Aim to get under the median of 9 seconds to have 10 players goal-side (a suggestion, based on this match's median). Tie it to the 9 of 10 tracked turnovers where we were caught short.
3. **Marking their top scorers.** Assign a named marker to C. Basquel and C. O'Callaghan, based on their 0-7 and 1-5.
4. **Ball security in the attacking centre.** Review the 7 turnovers lost there, including the stray passes by Langan, Roarty (extra time 2 85') and Campbell (extra time 1 80+1').
5. **Free-taking and discipline.** Review the 11 frees conceded, with the 3 by Murphy and the black-card situations.
