## 3. Tactical Analysis

**Kickouts.** Our kickouts split into 21 kept and 18 lost. In the first half we kept 6 of 12 (4 of 5 to our right, 1 of 5 to our left). In the second half it was 10 of 17, 5 of 7 to our left. In extra time we kept 5 of 10. Kickouts start a large share of possessions and wing kickouts create scoring chances more often than central ones (GAA Tactical Periodisation Model, p.6). Dublin's kickouts were won by their midfielders and full forward (C. Kilkenny 6, T. Clancy 5, B. Howard 4). We won only 7 of their 27.

**Ball movement and chains.** From the carry data (high confidence), 332 carries and 284 passes were logged. We had 47 possession chains, of which 19 ended in a score, 11 in a turnover and 13 in a missed shot; the other 4 ended in other ways. Of our scoring chains, 7 were direct and 12 were build-up. Scoring chains averaged 7.2 carriers. Most carriers made positive ground. Mogan (10.0 m per carry), McGonagle (15.9 m), Hugh McFadden (16.6 m on 6 carries) and Ryan McHugh (8.8 m) led the way, while Shea Malone averaged -0.1 m. The most productive link was Shane O'Donnell to Peadar Mogan, 9 passes, followed by Langan to O'Donnell (7) and Mogan to O'Donnell (7). The O'Donnell-Mogan-Langan triangle fed most of our long chains, and the right-side pairing of Conor O'Donnell and Langan also linked 6 times in that direction.

**How Dublin moved the ball.** The opposition carry data shows 406 logged carries and 57 chains (24 scoring, 12 turnover, 16 missed shot). Their scoring chains averaged 7.6 carriers, and 18 were build-up and 6 direct. The ball-path log shows 58 possessions, a median of 22 seconds and an average of 40.5 seconds. 24 of their possessions lasted over 30 seconds and 22 were 10 seconds or less. 34 worked into our defensive third, most often via our right (15) and the centre (11). Their main link was C. Kilkenny to B. Howard (12 passes), and N. Scully to C. O'Callaghan (11). The principle for these long possessions is that defenders should not get drawn to the ball and leave an opponent free to continue the move (Coaching Defensive Play, p.5).

**Territory.** Dublin had 58.9% of the possession to our 41.1%, so we had less of the ball. Our scoring zones were 16 from 23 inside the 45 centre (70%) and 4 from 8 inside the 45 right (50%), and none from 2 on the left. Dublin were 19 from 32 in the centre (59%).

**Shot selection.** Our shots by range show inside 20 m scoring 6 from 8 (1.25 points per shot), 20-40 m scoring 10 from 18 (0.56 per shot), and 40 m plus scoring 4 from 8 (1.0 per shot). Overall we scored 28 points from 34 shots (0.82 per shot). Shots at range were worth nearly as much as close-in ones, and the 20-40 m band was the weakest. A two-pointer is worth two points when kicked from outside the 40 m arc (GAA football rules, p.47).

**Personnel and position changes.** These are small samples. Ryan McHugh on for Max Campbell at half-time: in the 10 minutes before we scored 4 points to Dublin's 5, and in the 10 minutes after we scored 3 to their 3. That is too little to call. Oisín Gallen for Shea Malone at 2nd half 35': 0-0 either side in the 10 minutes before and 3 points each in the 10 minutes after. Ciarán Thompson for Conor O'Donnell (2nd half 61') had only 2 minutes after it, too early to judge. Sean Martin for Hugh McFadden at 2nd half 63' saw both teams score 4 points either side. The extra-time position move (Dáire Ó Baoill to centre half forward, Langan to midfield at extra time 1 71') had Dublin scoring 4 points to our 2 in the next 10 minutes, though the sample is small and we were chasing.

## 6. Areas for Improvement

**Own kickouts.** We lost 18 of 39 kickouts, and Dublin scored 9 times (14 points) from them, with 4 scores from 5 in extra time. This is the biggest-sample finding in the match. We need a clearer plan for length and side, because we kept 4 of 5 to our right in the first half but only 2 of 2 there in the second, and the centre kept only 3 of 8 in the second half. A second-half or single-channel figure is a pointer only.

**Ball security in the attacking centre.** The zone count shows 7 turnovers lost against 1 won in the attacking centre channel, and Langan's 3 possession losses (2 led to a Dublin score within 3 minutes, carry-data measure) and 2 stray passes came mainly there. The unforced errors were Langan (2 stray passes), Roarty (a stray pass at extra time 2 85'), Campbell (a stray pass at extra time 1 80+1') and McHugh (overcarrying at 2nd half 56').

**Getting goal-side after losing it.** Of the 10 GPS-tracked turnovers lost, 9 caught us short and the median time to get 10 players goal-side was 9.0 seconds, with 4 not recovered inside 15 seconds.

**Shot quality from 20-40 m.** This band scored 10 from 18 for 0.56 points per shot, against 1.25 inside 20 m and 1.0 from 40 m plus. We hit 8 wides, 3 shorts, a hit post, a save and a missed 45, 14 missed shots in all (the summary's missed shots total). Conor O'Donnell and Oisín Gallen each had 2 wides.

**Discipline.** We conceded 11 frees (Murphy 3, McHugh 2), and Dublin scored from 2 of them. Murphy and McGee took black cards at extra time 1 70'. This is a watch item, not an automatic sanction.

## 7. Training Recommendations

1. **Kickout restarts** (from the 18 kickouts lost, 9 of Dublin's 25 scores). Run kickout practice under contest with a set break-ball unit around the landing zones, and track kept rates by side and length over the full session, not by single channel.
2. **Ball security in the attacking centre** (7 lost against 1 won there). Small-sided possession work in the zone between the 45 and the 40m arc, with a stray-pass count for the forward line and midfield.
3. **Getting goal-side after a turnover** (median 9.0 seconds, 4 of 10 not recovered inside 15 seconds). Lose-it/win-it drills (Coaching Defensive Play, p.5) where defenders reset to an opponent rather than chase the ball. As a suggestion, aim to bring the median under 9 seconds.
4. **Shooting from 20-40 m** (0.56 points per shot). Shooting practice under pressure from that band, with the option of taking one more pass inside, compared with the two-point range, where we got 1.0 per shot.
5. **Extra-time conditioning** (running per minute fell from 1,541 m in the first half to 1,358 m in extra time 1). Add match-simulation blocks that run past 70 minutes for the players who played all 94.
